0002 · Proposed replacement: group reports by cause

owner victorwhite5 proposed reviewed 2026-11-01 · 0 days ago

Context

Decision 0001 accepted subject-only merging. The review of #4471 shows why identical words can conceal different mechanisms. This proposal preserves the accepted decision and recommends replacing its rule; it does not claim the desk has adopted it.

Proposed decision

Use subject text to search, then compare the underlying failure before merging. For the date reports, look up tenant locale and booking form and retain the channel. Do not classify by browser locale or intake tag alone. Use the executable cause rules for the export’s grouping, and retain contradictory report bodies as uncertainty requiring investigation.

Merge only when the cause matches. Keep parser and booking-v3 hint work in separate issues, with report IDs and one count per unique report. When tenant details or the mechanism are missing, leave the cause unplaced and request original input, displayed hint, stored timestamp and timezone evidence.
